| HyperFrames | 0.8.150 | Apache-2.0. Commercial use permitted, with license/notice obligations for software redistribution and applicable modification notices. |

No legal determination about the user's commercial entity is made here. Keep the tool licenses with redistributed editable software projects and recheck Remotion eligibility when moving this evaluation into commercial production.
