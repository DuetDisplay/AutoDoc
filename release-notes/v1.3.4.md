# A Better Ask AI

AutoDoc introduces a faster Ask AI beta with links to meeting moments, plus recording and privacy fixes.

AutoDoc now makes it easier to find answers in your meetings and return to the moments behind them.

- **Ask about your meetings:** Search across your meeting notes and transcripts, or open Ask AI directly from a recording to focus on that meeting.
- **Go back to the moment:** Open a source link to jump to the matching point in a recording.
- **Keep the conversation going:** Ask follow-up questions about the same meeting and get replies in the language of your question.
- **More reliable and private:** This release also improves recording safety, model downloads, and crash-report privacy. See the [full changelog](https://github.com/DuetDisplay/AutoDoc/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

**Ask AI is in beta** and is available on computers with a supported GPU and notes model. Recording, transcription, and notes remain available on other supported computers.
